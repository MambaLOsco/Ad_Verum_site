FAQ

What do I need to send?

An MT5/MT4 account statement (HTML), an MT5 Strategy Tester report, a Myfxbook/cTrader export, or a CSV with at least a close time and profit per trade. Ideally 300+ trades spanning 6+ months — more is better. We never need your EA code, indicator logic, or account credentials.

Will you steal my strategy?

We never see your strategy — only its trades. Trade history alone cannot reconstruct entry logic. Your data is used for your report only and deleted on request.

What if the verdict is "no edge"?
